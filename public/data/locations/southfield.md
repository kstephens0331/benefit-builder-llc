## Why Southfield employers use Section 125

Southfield sits in Oakland County, just northwest of Detroit, and serves as one of the region's most concentrated business centers. The city is dense with professional services firms, corporate offices, and healthcare employers, and that mix of office payrolls is exactly where a Section 125 cafeteria plan does its best work. These employers run steady, salaried payrolls with predictable pay periods, which makes pre-tax benefit deductions simple to apply consistently across the workforce.

A Section 125 cafeteria plan lets employees pay for qualified benefits with pre-tax dollars before income and payroll taxes are calculated. In Southfield, that matters more than it does in many other states. Michigan applies a flat 4.05% state income tax, and because the state offers no standard deduction at the income tax level, that rate reaches the first dollar of wages. Stack that on top of federal income tax and the 7.65% FICA that comes out of every paycheck, and the combined tax load on ordinary wages is significant. Every dollar an employee routes through a cafeteria plan is a dollar that escapes that layered burden, and the employer's share of FICA drops on the same dollars. For Southfield employers managing professional payrolls, those savings add up across a full roster.

## Section 125 for Southfield's office employers

Professional services firms and corporate offices in Southfield tend to run salaried payrolls with consistent pay frequencies, which suits a Section 125 plan cleanly. There are no seasonal swings or irregular hours to reconcile, so the pre-tax deductions stay stable from one pay period to the next and the administration stays straightforward.

The employer FICA savings from a cafeteria plan scale directly with participation. The more employees who enroll and the more they elect, the larger the employer's reduction in matching FICA. That is why employee education is not a side detail but a driver of results. When employees understand how the plan lowers their taxable wages and increases their take-home pay, enrollment rises, and the employer's savings rise with it. Southfield employers get a plan that works on paper and in practice.
