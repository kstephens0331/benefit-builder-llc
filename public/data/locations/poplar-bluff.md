## Why Poplar Bluff employers use Section 125

Poplar Bluff is the seat of Butler County, set in the Ozark foothills of southeast Missouri, and it works as a regional hub for healthcare, education, and small business across the surrounding area. Employers here run steady local payrolls, and that is exactly where a Section 125 cafeteria plan earns its place. A Section 125 plan lets workers move eligible premiums and contributions into pre-tax elections, which raises take-home pay without raising gross wages. Because those pre-tax dollars come out before payroll taxes, the employer's FICA obligation drops on every covered paycheck, pay period after pay period.

For Poplar Bluff employers, the appeal is the combination of real savings and zero added workload. The pre-tax benefits sit on top of payroll you already run, and the administration is handled for you. The same logic carries to nearby employers in Dexter, Doniphan, and Sikeston, where the same regional industries and steady payrolls make a cafeteria plan a straightforward fit. When dozens of paychecks each carry a pre-tax election, the FICA savings add up across the year while employees keep more of what they earn.
