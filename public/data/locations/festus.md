## Why Festus employers use Section 125

Festus sits in Jefferson County, Missouri, about 30 miles south of St. Louis. Together with the adjoining Twin City of Crystal City, it anchors a corner of the southern St. Louis metro. The local economy runs on small businesses, healthcare providers, and the trades, the kind of employers who compete for skilled workers and feel every payroll-tax dollar. A Section 125 cafeteria plan fits that reality. When an employee makes a modest pre-tax election, the amount comes out of pay before federal income tax and FICA are calculated. The worker keeps more take-home pay on the same gross wage, and the employer pays less in matching FICA on every dollar that runs through the plan. The savings are real on both sides, and the administration is handled for you.

For Festus employers, that combination matters. A pre-tax benefits program helps a small shop offer something competitive without raising wages, and it does so with a predictable monthly cost. Employers in neighboring Crystal City, Herculaneum, and Arnold use the same structure for the same reasons. The plan works across pay frequencies and across the full range of Jefferson County industries, from a dental office to a plumbing contractor to a family-owned retailer.
