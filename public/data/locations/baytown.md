## Why Baytown employers use Section 125

Baytown sits on the northern shore of Galveston Bay in eastern Harris County, along the Houston Ship Channel's industrial corridor. The local economy is anchored by large industrial employers, manufacturers, healthcare providers, and small businesses, with a workforce that spans both hourly and salaried roles. That mix is exactly where a Section 125 cafeteria plan does the most good.

A pre-tax election lets each worker route part of their pay toward qualified benefits before federal tax and FICA are calculated. For the employee, that lower taxable wage means more take-home pay, which functions like a real raise without changing the gross. For the employer, every dollar an employee elects pre-tax also reduces the wages subject to the company's 7.65% FICA match, so payroll tax drops on each enrolled worker. Because Texas has no state income tax, all of the savings for Baytown employers and their teams come from federal income tax and FICA rather than any state withholding.

The same logic applies to nearby employers in La Porte, Channelview, and Mont Belvieu, where industrial and manufacturing payrolls dominate. For these companies, pre-tax benefits are a low-cost way to raise net pay and trim employer payroll tax at the same time.
